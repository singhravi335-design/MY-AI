Sampling Visualizer

No API call needed. See how the three sampling knobs reshape the odds of the next word.

Green = still allowed. Struck-through grey = removed by Top-K or Top-P. Percentages are renormalised after filtering. Illustrative numbers, not Gemini's real ones.

Temperature

Scales the randomness of every word choice. Near 0 the top word nearly always wins. Above 1 unlikely words get a real chance. Facts: low. Poetry: higher.

Top-P (nucleus)

Keep the smallest group of words whose probabilities add up to P, drop the rest. 0.9 means ignore the unlikely 10% tail.

Top-K

Only the K most likely words are candidates. K = 1 is greedy: always the single best word.

Max output tokens

A hard ceiling on answer length (about 4 characters per token). The model does not plan to fit; it just gets cut off, and finishReason shows MAX_TOKENS.

Stop sequences

Generation ends the moment this text would appear. The stop text itself is not included.

Penalties

Presence penalty discourages words already used at all. Frequency penalty grows with each repeat. Useful against repetitive loops. Some models reject them.

Seed

A fixed seed with identical settings makes runs more repeatable. Not a strict guarantee.
